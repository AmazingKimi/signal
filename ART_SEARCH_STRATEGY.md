# Art Search Strategy

The highest-priority art sources are Sotheby's, Christie's, Artsy, Phillips, LiveAuctioneers and Wright. They receive explicit `site:` queries first and a P0 ranking signal, but P0 never means authenticity, value, price fairness or buying advice.

P0 coverage is additive. Every rich art plan also contains unrestricted general-web searches, sale and inquiry intent, upcoming-auction terms, historical sales and price clues, years, materials/features and multilingual variants. Ordinary Settings does not expose the internal source list or backend controls.

SIGNAL consumes only the public title, snippet, URL and optional thumbnail returned by the configured search backend. It never requests a result destination to enrich or classify it. Historical results remain market context and cannot become live opportunities.
